Forever Rogue: a do-everything Rogue companion for WoW Forever

November 19, 2026 · 3 min read

Forever Rogue is new: a free, open source Rogue companion for World of Warcraft: Forever. It works for every Rogue race and both factions, and it was built around Rip, a Human Rogue of the Alliance who started out in Northshire Valley. Version 0.1.0 came out today, and 0.1.1 followed shortly after with pickpocket spots on the minimap.

It’s the fifth of the family, after Andric’s Addon for Priests, Parzelle’s Addon for Mages, Tristan’s Addon for Paladins and Tazu’s Addon for Shamans, so the window will feel familiar if you’ve used any of them.

Pockets, boxes and locks

A Rogue’s coin comes from other people’s pockets, so that’s where Forever Rogue starts. Type /frogue.

  • Pick Pocket log: every pickpocket with its coin, items, junkbox tier, mob, level and zone. You get totals for the session, the day and your lifetime, copper per pickpocket, pickpockets per hour, your best mobs, and a refill timer per mob so you know when a pocket is worth trying again.
  • Best targets: pickpocket spots by level and zone. Your own results sit next to community data from GoldScout players, shown with sample sizes and marked unconfirmed.
  • Minimap pins (new in 0.1.1): pins for spots near your level that the research has map coordinates for, plus every spot where you’ve picked a pocket yourself. Hover one for the mob, your earnings there and the distance. Spots without sourced coordinates get no pin; nothing is guessed. Turn them off with /frogue pins.
  • Junkboxes and Lockpicking: the boxes in your bags and bank, whether you can open them, and what they’re worth, using Auctionator prices when it’s installed and vendor prices otherwise. There’s also a lockpicking route and the locked dungeon doors.

Poisons and the Rogue bar

Poisons shows what’s on each weapon (in Forever a poison and a sharpening stone can share one), your stock, and the suggested poison for each hand. The Rogue bar has a Pick Lock button that opens the next box you can, main-hand and off-hand poison buttons, reagent counts and a pocket counter. One press is one action, out of combat only, and no keys are bound by default.

Everything else a Rogue needs

  • Rotation: Solo, Dungeon and Raid priorities for Assassination, Combat and Subtlety, using only spells you know.
  • Talents: seven Icy Veins builds laid out on Forever’s own Rogue trees, each exactly 51 points.
  • Gear and bags: upgrades for your level, plus the next bag upgrade for your race and faction.
  • Leveling: a path, trainer visits and the class quests for your race and faction.
  • Gold: each session broken down by source, from pickpocket coin to quests.
  • PvP: honor and rank where the game lets addons read it, battlegrounds, Rogue PvP notes and poison choices.
  • Learn the Rogue, plus reminders for poisons, reagents, Thieves’ Tools and class quests.

Forever doesn’t give addons the combat log and hides some values in combat. Inside dungeons it hides mob names too, so those pickpockets are logged as “unknown mob”. When something can’t be read, the addon says so instead of guessing.

Get it

On Linux, OmaForge installs it and keeps it updated:

omaforge install -c forever github:ninepointlabs/forever-rogue

On any other system, download ForeverRogue-v0.1.1.zip from the v0.1.1 release, unzip it into _forever_/Interface/AddOns/, type /reload, then /frogue. It’s for WoW Forever only, and it hasn’t been tested in game yet, so please report anything odd (especially pins that don’t line up with the minimap) on the issue tracker. Every fact carries its source and the date it was checked. It’s an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard. Questions go to help@ninepointlabs.com.
